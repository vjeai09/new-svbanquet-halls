## The Problem with Traditional Banquet Halls

### Forced Package Model (What Competitors Do)

```
Hall rental: ₹30,000-50,000
```

**Why it costs more:**

- Hidden fees (kitchen usage, decoration setup, overtime charges)

**Result:** You pay ₹2,50,000-3,50,000 for a 200-guest event, stuck with vendors you may not trust.
