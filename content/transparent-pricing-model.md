## Planning Your Budget

### Sample Budget Framework (150 Guests)

**Your direct costs:**
- **Hall rental:** Call for quote based on your needs
- **Caterer:** ₹400-800/plate × 150 = ₹60,000-1,20,000 (you negotiate)
- **Decorator:** ₹30,000-80,000 (based on complexity, you choose)
- **Photographer:** ₹20,000-60,000 (based on experience, you decide)

**Total event cost:** Depends entirely on YOUR choices

**Budget control tips:**
1. Get 3-4 quotes from caterers (prices vary widely)
2. Negotiate for bulk orders
3. Morning events cost less than evening (breakfast vs dinner)
4. DIY decorations reduce costs
5. Shop around for all vendors
