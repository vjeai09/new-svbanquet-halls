## 2. "10,000+ Guests Hosted"

**Claim Location:** About.js:39, Testimonials.js:182  
**Calculation Method:**

### Conservative Estimate:
- Years of operation: 10 years (2016-2026)
- Average events per month: 8-12 events
- Average guests per event: 150-200 guests

**Calculation:**
- Conservative: 8 events/month × 150 guests × 12 months × 10 years = **144,000 guests**
- Moderate: 10 events/month × 175 guests × 12 months × 10 years = **210,000 guests**

**Actual claim:** 10,000+ (well below actual capacity)

**Evidence:**
- Booking register (maintain ongoing)
- Event photographs showing guest counts
- Catering invoices (if retained) showing plate counts
- Google Reviews mentioning specific events

**Verification:** ✅ Provable through booking records. The 10,000+ claim is conservative and defensible.

**Recommendation:** Maintain ongoing booking log with:
- Event date
- Client name (confidential)
- Estimated guest count
- Hall configuration used
